<sup>1</sup> Sha’ul and Timothy, servants of יהושע Messiah, to all the set-apart ones in Messiah יהושע who are in Philippi, with the overseers and attendants:

<sup>2</sup> Favour to you and peace from Elohim our Father and the Master יהושע Messiah.

<sup>3</sup> I thank my Elohim in all my remembrance of you.

<sup>4</sup> In all my prayers for all of you, I always pray with joy,

<sup>5</sup> for your fellowship in the Good News from the first day until now,

<sup>6</sup> being persuaded of this, that He who has begun a good work in you shall perfect it until the day of יהושע Messiah.

<sup>7</sup> It is right for me to think this of you all, because I have you in my heart, all of you being sharers of the favour with me, both in my chains and in the defence and confirmation of the Good News.

<sup>8</sup> For Elohim is my witness, how I long for you all with the affection of יהושע Messiah.

<sup>9</sup> And this I pray, that your love might extend more and more in knowledge and all discernment,

<sup>10</sup> for you to examine the matters that differ, in order to be sincere, and not stumbling, until the day of Messiah,

<sup>11</sup> being filled with the fruit of righteousness, through יהושע Messiah, to the esteem and praise of Elohim.

<sup>12</sup> And I wish you to know, brothers, that what has befallen me has turned out for the advancement of the Good News,

<sup>13</sup> so that it has become known to all the palace guard, and to all the rest, that my chains are in Messiah;

<sup>14</sup> and most of the brothers, trusting in the Master because of my chains, are much more bold to fearlessly speak the word.

<sup>15</sup> Some indeed proclaim Messiah even from envy and strife, but some also out of pleasure –

<sup>16</sup> the former announce Messiah from selfish ambition, not sincerely, thinking to add distress to my chains,

<sup>17</sup> but the latter out of love, knowing that I am appointed for the defence of the Good News.

<sup>18</sup> What then? Only that in every way, whether in pretence or in truth, Messiah is announced. And in this I rejoice, in fact, I shall rejoice.

<sup>19</sup> For I know that this shall turn out for my deliverance through your prayer and the supply of the Spirit of יהושע Messiah,

<sup>20</sup> according to my intense longing and anticipation that I shall not be ashamed at all, but that with all boldness, as always, so now also Messiah shall be made great in my body, whether by life or by death.

<sup>21</sup> For to me, to live is Messiah, and to die is gain.

<sup>22</sup> And if to live in the flesh is to me a fruit of work, then what shall I choose? I do not know.

<sup>23</sup> For I am pressed down by the two, having a desire to depart and be with Messiah, which is much better,

<sup>24</sup> but to remain in the flesh is more necessary for your sake.

<sup>25</sup> And being persuaded of this, I know that I shall stay and continue with you all, for your progress and joy of belief,

<sup>26</sup> so that your boasting might overflow in יהושע Messiah on account of me, through my coming to you again.

<sup>27</sup> Only, behave yourselves worthily of the Good News of Messiah, in order that whether I come and see you or am absent, I hear about you, that you stand fast in one spirit, with one being, striving together for the belief of the Good News,

<sup>28</sup> without being frightened in any way by those who oppose, which to them truly is a proof of destruction, but to you of deliverance, and that from Elohim.

<sup>29</sup> Because to you it has been given as a favour, on behalf of Messiah, not only to believe in Him, but also to suffer for His sake,

<sup>30</sup> having the same struggle which you saw in me, and now hear to be in me.
