<sup>1</sup> If, then, there is any encouragement in Messiah, if any comfort of love, if any fellowship of Spirit, if any affection and compassion,

<sup>2</sup> make my joy complete by being of the same mind, having the same love, one in being and of purpose,

<sup>3</sup> doing none at all through selfishness or self-conceit, but in humility consider others better than yourselves.

<sup>4</sup> Each one should look out not only for his own interests, but also for the interests of others.

<sup>5</sup> For, let this mind be in you which was also in Messiah יהושע,

<sup>6</sup> who, being in the form of Elohim, did not regard equality with Elohim a matter to be grasped,

<sup>7</sup> but emptied Himself, taking the form of a servant, and came to be in the likeness of men.

<sup>8</sup> And having been found in fashion as a man, He humbled Himself and became obedient unto death, death even of a stake.

<sup>9</sup> Elohim, therefore, has highly exalted Him and given Him the Name which is above every name,

<sup>10</sup> that at the Name of יהושע every knee should bow, of those in heaven, and of those on earth, and of those under the earth,

<sup>11</sup> and every tongue should confess that יהושע Messiah is Master, to the esteem of Elohim the Father.

<sup>12</sup> So that, my beloved, as you always obeyed – not only in my presence, but now much rather in my absence – work out your own deliverance with fear and trembling,

<sup>13</sup> for it is Elohim who is working in you both to desire and to work for His good pleasure.

<sup>14</sup> Do all matters without grumblings and disputings,

<sup>15</sup> in order that you be blameless and faultless, children of Elohim without blemish in the midst of a crooked and perverse generation, among whom you shine as lights in the world,

<sup>16</sup> holding on to the Word of life, for a boast to me in the day of Messiah, that I have not run in vain or laboured in vain.

<sup>17</sup> In fact, even if I am being poured out as a drink offering on the offering and service of your belief, I am glad and rejoice with you all.

<sup>18</sup> So you too should be glad and rejoice with me.

<sup>19</sup> But I trust in the Master יהושע to send Timothy to you shortly, so that I too am encouraged by news from you.

<sup>20</sup> For I have no one else of the same mind, who takes a genuine interest in your welfare.

<sup>21</sup> For all seek their own interests, not those of Messiah יהושע.

<sup>22</sup> But you know he has proven himself, that as a son with his father he served with me for the Good News.

<sup>23</sup> So I expect to send him as soon as I see how it goes with me,

<sup>24</sup> and I trust in the Master that I myself shall also come shortly.

<sup>25</sup> But I thought it necessary to send to you Epaphroditos, my brother, fellow worker, and fellow soldier, and your emissary and servant to my need,

<sup>26</sup> since he was longing for you all, and being troubled because you had heard that he was sick.

<sup>27</sup> For indeed he was sick, near to death, but Elohim had compassion on him, and not only on him but on me as well, lest I should have sadness upon sadness.

<sup>28</sup> Therefore I sent him more eagerly, so that on seeing him again you might rejoice, and I be less sad.

<sup>29</sup> Receive him therefore in the Master with all joy, and hold such as he in esteem,

<sup>30</sup> because for the work of Messiah he was near death, risking his life, to fill up what was lacking in your service toward me.
